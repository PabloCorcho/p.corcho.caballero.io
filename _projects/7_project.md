[Hector](https://hector.survey.org.au) is the next major dark-time instrument for the Anglo-Australian Telescope (AAT): a multi integral-field-unit spectrograph aimed at obtaining a low-redshift galaxy survey of up to 15,000 galaxies.

I am involved in this Integral Field Spectroscopy survey, which will provide new insights into galaxy evolution.
