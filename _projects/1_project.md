I am the main developer of **PyKOALA**, a Python library for reducing Integral Field Spectroscopy (IFS) data. It is the primary tool used to process data from the [KOALA](https://aat.anu.edu.au/science/instruments/current/koala/overview) instrument on the Anglo-Australian Telescope (AAT).

PyKOALA turns raw IFU exposures into science-ready spectral data cubes, providing the spatially- and spectrally-resolved data that underlies my research on galaxy quenching and star formation.
