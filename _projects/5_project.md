I am a member of the [Euclid](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Euclid) mission, a space telescope launched by the European Space Agency to survey about 14,000 square degrees of the sky in order to constrain the nature of dark energy.

My role involves quality control of ground-based ancillary data (0.5 FTE, OU-EXT), which complements the observations made from space, alongside independent research on galaxy evolution using Euclid data.
