**BESTA** (Bayesian Estimator for Stellar Populations Analysis) is a software tool I developed to infer the physical properties of galaxies using Bayesian inference and Monte Carlo techniques, combining stellar population synthesis models with observational data to constrain quantities such as stellar age, metallicity, and star-formation history.
